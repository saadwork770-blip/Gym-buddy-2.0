AI Coaching · Version 2.0

Your plan, re-written every week around what you actually did.

GymBuddy 2.0 keeps everything from version 1 — the full Fitness Time program, the exercise library with real photos and animated demonstrations, the local profile — and adds a coaching engine on top. It picks your next weights from the reps and effort you logged, rebuilds the split around the days you can train, and swaps exercises when something stalls or starts to hurt.

What is new in 2.0

Four things the first version could not do

It picks your weights

Double progression, RPE autoregulation and effort-adjusted 1RM tracking decide the next load — and every jump is rounded to what the machine can actually select. It tells you the reasoning, with the reps and RPE that produced it.

It rebuilds your week

Tell it which days you can train and it re-chooses the split, re-orders the sessions so overlapping muscle work lands as far apart as the week allows, and re-sizes each session to the time you have.

It changes the exercises

Stalled for three sessions? Missing equipment? A joint that hurts? The coach substitutes a movement that trains the same pattern, explains the swap, and leaves the decision with you.

It runs the session with you

A live workout player calls the load for every set, times your rest, reads your readiness check-in, and recalculates the next session's prescriptions before you have left the building.

How the coaching actually works

No black box

What this is, and what it is not. The coaching engine is a transparent rule system built on well-established training principles — double progression, RPE-based autoregulation, weekly volume landmarks and mesocycle periodization. It is not a medical device and it cannot see you train. Every recommendation shows its reasoning so you can override it, and you should: if a load feels wrong, it is wrong. All data stays in this browser's local storage — no account, no server, nothing transmitted. Photos and animated demonstrations come from free-exercise-db (public domain, Unlicense) and are stored in this project, so the site works fully offline. If an exercise causes joint pain rather than muscle fatigue, flag it in the library and see a physio — not an app.
